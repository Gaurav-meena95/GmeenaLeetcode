## Problem Description
Exun Leaderboard

The Exun quiz leaderboard has no ties. Your team is ranked XXX-th in the leaderboard, and your rival team is ranked YYY-th.

Find how many teams are ranked strictly between you and your rival.

Input Format

- The first and only line of input contains 222 integers - XXX and YYY.

Output Format

Output the number of teams that are ranked strictly between you and your rival.

Constraints

- 1≤X,Y≤101 \le X, Y \le 101≤X,Y≤10

- X≠YX \ne YX=Y

Sample 1:
InputOutput
```
1 5
```

```
3
```

Explanation:

Your team is ranked 111, and your rival's team is ranked 555. There are 333 teams (222nd, 333rd and 444th) between you.

Sample 2:
InputOutput
```
2 1
```

```
0
```

Explanation:

Your rival team is 111st, and you are 222nd, no teams between you.
